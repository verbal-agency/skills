# Design Learning Practice

Create evidence-producing practice from capabilities and concept units. Do not
count activity, confidence, or exposure as mastery.

## Required inputs

Read the target capabilities, relevant graph neighborhood, authored concept
units, learner level, available environment, and delivery constraints. Read
[references/practice-design.md](references/practice-design.md) before designing
a substantial exercise set.

## Workflow

1. **Define the evidence.** For each capability, state what observable response
   or artifact would demonstrate success and what common weak evidence would
   not.
2. **Choose cognitive levels.** Use only the required levels from `recognize`,
   `recall`, `explain`, `discriminate`, `apply`, `diagnose`, `design`, and
   `defend`. Avoid dressing a recall item in a long scenario.
3. **Create diagnostic items first.** Probe prerequisites and known
   misconceptions before teaching or giving hints.
4. **Build a practice progression.** Move from retrieval and worked examples to
   completion tasks, independent application, transfer, failure diagnosis, and
   design where appropriate.
5. **Use contrasts and near misses.** Ask learners to distinguish confusable
   concepts and explain why plausible alternatives fail.
6. **Design authentic tasks.** Mirror the decisions, artifacts, tools,
   constraints, and failure modes of the real capability.
7. **Specify labs completely.** Include setup, starting state, task, observable
   success, expected failure, hints, cleanup, cost or safety limits, and a
   no-access alternative when practical.
8. **Separate prompts from answers.** Store solutions, scoring criteria, and
   feedback rules outside learner-facing prompts. Avoid answer-shaped hints.
9. **Write analytic rubrics.** Score the material dimensions of performance,
   including reasoning and boundary conditions, rather than keyword matching.
10. **Tag metadata.** Record concept IDs, capability IDs, level, prerequisites,
    estimated effort, environment, volatility, and source claim IDs.
11. **Write the set.** Copy and adapt
    [assets/exercise-set-template.yaml](assets/exercise-set-template.yaml).

## Validation

Check that every item measures its declared capability, has enough information
for a qualified learner, admits the documented answer, avoids accidental answer
leakage, and can be administered repeatedly or has variant guidance. Execute
technical labs where safe and feasible.

Report capability coverage, difficulty distribution, environment dependencies,
unvalidated labs, and the evidence each item produces.
